Description of service

SnowbirdDays is a residency-day tracker for iPhone and iPad. It uses iOS background location, on-device storage, and a daily hash chain to produce a cryptographic-consistency record of the days you spent in each US state and foreign country. The records are intended to help you reason about state-tax and similar jurisdictional residency questions.

The app is not a substitute for professional advice. Day counts are observations of where the device was, not legal determinations. Residency rules differ by state and country and depend on facts well beyond day counts (domicile, intent, source of income, family ties). Consult a CPA, tax attorney, or audit professional before making any tax, legal, or financial decision.
